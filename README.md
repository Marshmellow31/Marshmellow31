### 🛰️ Research Focus: Counter-UAS

Conducting simulation-backed research on defensive algorithms for autonomous aerial swarms:

* **RAC-SRPL**: Randomized authenticated anchor audits designed to detect formation-preserving GNSS spoofing attacks and tightly bound absolute swarm displacement.
* **VERGE-CUAS**: Uncertainty-aware, collision-conscious trajectory planning and clearance for partially responsive drone swarms inside restricted perimeters.
* *Methodology*: Strict computational verification through seeded Monte Carlo simulations (including an extensive 7,100-trial experimental study), maintaining clear demarcation between simulation boundaries and hardware validation claims.
